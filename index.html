MEGA

A personal desktop email assistant.

MEGA is a private, single-user Windows application that brings its owner's Gmail accounts into one inbox. It sorts and prioritizes mail, summarizes what needs attention, helps draft replies, and is being extended to work with Google Contacts and Google Calendar.

It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a hosted service, it is not offered to the public, and there is nothing here to sign up for.
